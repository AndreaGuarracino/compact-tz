Compact Time Zones

    • ⋯ opens search: city, country, IANA zone, or EST/PST/CET.
    • Double-click a name to rename it. Enter saves, Esc cancels.
    • Hover: full name and IANA zone.
    • Reorder: drag, Alt+↑/↓, or ⇅ by offset.
    • Keys: ↑/↓ select, Enter rename, Delete remove.
    • × then "Remove?" to delete.
    • Date and slider set the time. Now resets.
    • Green 09-18, grey 22-08, blue row is yours.
